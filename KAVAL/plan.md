## 1. Thesis (locked)

The brief asks for a parcel-level crop classifier at >95% accuracy. We measured the
supplied data. **That target is not reachable without validation leakage**, and the
brief's own clause — *"spatial or parcel leakage between training and evaluation data
will invalidate the claimed result"* — is the reason why.

So we do not build a classifier and chase the number. We build the thing the number
was a proxy for.

**The classifier is the instrument. The product is the audit.**

India's Digital Crop Survey sends surveyors to record crop, stage and a geotagged
photograph for every parcel, every season. Its output drives crop insurance, procurement
and compensation. It is expensive, recurring, and — as our forensics show — carries
provenance artefacts that no downstream system currently detects. KAVAL uses satellite
foundation-model embeddings to decide, per record: **trust it, re-verify it, or retire
the visit entirely.**

Three consequences that shape every decision below:

1. **Disagreement is the product**, not the error. A parcel where satellite and survey
   disagree is the most valuable row in the table.
2. **We report the honest number and show the dishonest one beside it.** The Leakage
   Ladder (§6, Phase 4) is a headline deliverable, not an appendix.
3. **Low accuracy on rare crops is a finding about the data**, not a modelling failure.
   Marigold's "36 samples" are 3 independent observations. We say so.

### 1.1 Locked architecture decisions

| # | Decision | Value |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Positioning | Audit-first. Classifier presented as instrument within it. |
| A2 | Accuracy stance | Confront the 95% target with measured evidence. Headline metric = capture-event-grouped. Never claim 95%. |
| A3 | Team | 3–4 members (§9) |
| A4 | Demo stack | React + Leaflet frontend, FastAPI backend |
| A5 | Data | Supplied files are a **small reference sample, not the working dataset**. Real data is acquired independently (§4). |
| A6 | AI depth | AI must be load-bearing, not decorative. Six AI components (§3). A map with a model behind it is an explicit non-goal. |

## 2. What we measured before proposing (Phase 0 — done)

Reproducible via `scripts/gt_forensics.py` and `scripts/leakage_ladder.py`.
Artefacts in `docs/evidence/`.

**Ground-truth forensics** (`docs/evidence/gt_forensics.md`):

- 14,643 survey records resolve to **3,204 distinct capture events** — 4.57× label inflation
- One capture event supplies up to **43 parcel labels**; 93.8% of records sit in a multi-parcel event
- **Zero** of the 3,204 events contain more than one crop label — the label is attached to the *event*, then copied
- Median distance from photograph to the parcel it documents: **1,436 m** (p90 3,808 m; 62.3% over 1 km)
- Per-crop effective n collapses: Maize 1,568 records → 162 events; Marigold 36 → 3; Mango 6 → 1
- 385 records declare more than one crop category; 645 store two coordinates in one cell; 13 geocode outside the study area
- **No cadastral parcel geometry was supplied.** Records are points.

**The Leakage Ladder** (`docs/evidence/leakage_ladder.md`) — same model, same features,
only the split changes. Numbers regenerated by the script; see the artefact for current values.

This is the evidence base the proposal stands on. A proposal carrying measurements
outranks a proposal carrying intentions.

## 3. The AI spine (six components)

Per A6, each component is load-bearing — remove it and the system stops working.

| # | Component | Why it must exist | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI-1** | **Parcel delineation from satellite.** Pretrained *Fields of The World* U-Net (EfficientNet-B7) over bi-temporal Sentinel-2 to segment field boundaries. | We were given points, not polygons. "Parcel-level" is impossible without them. Also squarely FarmwiseAI's cadastral business. | 2 |
| **AI-2** | **Satellite foundation-model embeddings.** Google Satellite Embedding / AlphaEarth (64-d, 10 m, annual) **and** Presto (NASA Harvest, time-series transformer), compared head to head. | The brief asks for exactly this comparison. Two different foundation models, two different inductive biases. | 3 |
| **AI-3** | **Multiple-Instance Learning over capture-event bags.** A capture event is a *bag* of parcels carrying one bag-level label. Attention-MIL head learns which members actually support the label. | This is the methodological core. The label defect becomes the modelling framework. Attention weight per parcel *is* the audit signal. | 5 |
| **AI-4** | **Confident learning + probability calibration.** Estimate P(observed label ≠ true label) per record; isotonic-calibrate class probabilities; report ECE alongside accuracy. | Converts model output into a defensible Record Trust Score. Uncalibrated confidence cannot route a budget. | 6 |
| **AI-5** | **Agentic analyst.** An LLM agent planning over typed tools (`query_parcels`, `crop_area_summary`, `compare_to_registry`, `confusion_report`, `route_verification_budget`, `export_layer`) to answer the brief's natural-language requests. | This is the product surface. It answers every "Comparable Analysis Request" in the brief, and it demonstrates the agentic direction FarmwiseAI's Task 1 is built around. | 7 |
| **AI-6** | **Vision-language photo verification** *(conditional)*. Run a VLM over the survey photograph to test whether it depicts the declared crop and stage. | Closes the audit loop with multimodal evidence. **Conditional** on FarmwiseAI supplying the images behind `image_path` — we hold paths, not pixels. | 6 |

**Non-goals (explicit).** Training a remote-sensing foundation model from scratch. Raw
imagery preprocessing beyond what AI-1 and AI-2 need. A CRUD dashboard. Any claim of
>95% accuracy.

## 5. System architecture

```
                     ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┐
   ACQUISITION       │ GEE · Presto · FTW · registry · boundaries │
                     └──────────────────┬────────────────────────┘
                                        ▼
   GEOMETRY          AI-1  field-boundary segmentation → parcel polygons
                                        ▼
   LINKAGE           point → polygon → survey number; capture-event bags
                                        ▼
   FEATURES          AI-2  GSE (64-d) ‖ Presto ‖ seasonal S1/S2 → feature store
                                        ▼
   LEARNING          AI-3  attention-MIL over bags   +   baseline heads
                                        ▼
   AUDIT             AI-4  confident learning · isotonic calibration
                     AI-6  VLM photo check (conditional)
                                        ▼
                          Record Trust Score  +  agreement status
                                        ▼
   ECONOMICS         verification routing under a budget of N visits
                                        ▼
   SURFACE           FastAPI  ←  AI-5 agentic analyst  →  React + Leaflet
```

**Agreement status** — the vocabulary the whole product speaks, replacing bare class labels:

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `CONFIRMED` | Satellite agrees with survey, calibrated confidence high | Auto-clear; skip next season's visit |
| `DISPUTED` | Satellite disagrees at high confidence | Top of the field worklist |
| `UNCERTAIN` | Model cannot separate; low confidence | Route by budget |
| `UNVERIFIABLE` | Provenance too weak to test (huge bag, distant photo, mixed crop) | Re-survey the *record*, not the parcel |
| `UNSEEN` | No survey record exists | Satellite-only prediction |

## 6. Phases

Each phase: owned by one agent, has deliverables and an acceptance gate. An agent may
not open the next phase until the gate passes. Gates are checked by
`reproducibility-guard`.

### Phase 0 — Evidence & Framing ✅ done
**Agent:** `gt-forensics` · **Deliverables:** `docs/evidence/gt_forensics.{json,md}`,
`docs/evidence/leakage_ladder.{json,md}`
**Gate:** every numeric claim in the proposal traces to a script in `scripts/`.

### Phase 1 — Data Acquisition & Provenance
**Agent:** `data-acquisition` · **Skills:** `gee-export`, `data-provenance`
- Register GEE account, authenticate, define Sivagiri AOI from supplied taluk polygon
- Acquire GSE annual embeddings 2024 + 2023 (change detection baseline)
- Acquire S2 L2A and S1 GRD seasonal composites for the Rabi 2024-25 window
- Acquire ERA5-Land / CHIRPS agro-climate; TN Season & Crop Report registry
- Repair the 645 multi-valued and 13 out-of-area coordinate records
- Write `data/PROVENANCE.md` — source, date, licence, transform, checksum per file
**Gate:** every file in `data/` appears in `PROVENANCE.md`; AOI matches supplied taluk boundary.

### Phase 2 — Parcel Delineation (AI-1)
**Agent:** `parcel-delineation` · **Skills:** `parcel-join`
- Run the pretrained FTW U-Net over bi-temporal S2 across the taluk
- Post-process to clean polygons; snap ground-truth points into them
- Measure: what fraction of the 14,643 points land in a delineated field, one point per field?
- Compare against supplied cadastral geometry **if** FarmwiseAI provides it — report IoU
**Gate (revised 2026-09-30 — architecture decision):** BOTH of
  * `match_rate` ≥ 80% — points falling inside exactly one polygon, **and**
  * `one_point_per_field_rate` ≥ 60% — fields containing exactly one survey point

The first condition alone is **degenerate**: it is trivially satisfied by
*under*-segmentation, since one polygon per village scores 100%. The risk register
anticipated FTW *over*-segmenting smallholder fields; the failure mode that passes the
naive gate is the opposite one. Phase 0 measured a median photo-to-parcel distance of
1,436 m, so survey points cluster away from the land they describe and many distinct
parcels will land in one field.

Consequences now handled structurally rather than by inspection:
  * **`field_id` is a leakage group key.** Parcels sharing a delineated polygon carry
    identical zonal features. `splits.headline_groups` unions `bag_id` with `field_id`
    via union-find, so transitively linked parcels (A shares a bag with B, B shares a
    field with C) never split across folds.
  * **Crop-area statistics must not be computed per parcel where a field is shared.**
    A polygon holding several records with conflicting labels has no defensible area
    figure; Phase 7 aggregates at field level for `geometry_source == 'ftw_shared'`.

### Phase 3 — Embeddings & Feature Store (AI-2)
**Agent:** `embedding-engineer` · **Skills:** `gee-export`, `parcel-join`
- Zonal-aggregate GSE over each parcel polygon (mean + std, justified in writing)
- Run Presto over per-parcel S1/S2/meteo time series to produce its embedding
- Build the feature store: parcel_id · geometry · GSE · Presto · seasonal · label · bag_id · provenance
**Gate:** feature store reproducible from `PROVENANCE.md` + scripts alone; no manual steps.

### Phase 4 — Leakage-Honest Baselines & the Ladder
**Agent:** `leakage-auditor` · **Skills:** `leakage-protocol`, `metrics-report`
- Re-run the Leakage Ladder on the *full acquired* feature store (Phase 0 ran it on the sample)
- Compare heads: LightGBM · Random Forest · logistic · MLP, on identical splits
- Compare sources: GSE · Presto · GSE+Presto · +seasonal
- Fix the headline protocol: **capture-event-grouped CV**, village-grouped reported beside it
**Gate:** no result is ever reported without its split regime named. `leakage-protocol`
compliance is mandatory for every downstream model.

### Phase 5 — Multiple-Instance Learning (AI-3)
**Agent:** `mil-modeler` · **Skills:** `leakage-protocol`, `metrics-report`
- Formalise capture events as bags; implement attention-MIL head over embeddings
- Compare MIL against the Phase 4 per-parcel baselines on identical event-grouped splits
- Extract per-parcel attention weights → `bag_support_score`
- Report honestly if MIL does not beat the baseline; the attention signal has audit value regardless
**Gate:** MIL evaluated on the same folds as Phase 4; attention weights exported per parcel.

### Phase 6 — Trust, Calibration & Verification Economics (AI-4, AI-6)
**Agent:** `calibration-trust`, then `verification-economics`
- Confident learning → P(label is wrong) per record
- Isotonic calibration fitted on validation only; report ECE before/after
- **Record Trust Score** = f(bag size, photo distance, bag_support_score, stage–crop plausibility, mixed-crop flag)
- Assign `agreement status` per §5
- VLM photo check if images arrive (AI-6)
- Budget router: given N visits, maximise expected corrections; produce the worklist
- Quantify the auto-clear saving in surveyor-days
**Gate:** calibration fitted strictly on validation; ECE reported; budget router has a
measurable objective, not a heuristic ranking.

### Phase 7 — API & Agentic Analyst (AI-5)
**Agent:** `api-engineer`, then `agent-analyst-builder` · **Skills:** `fastapi-contract`
- FastAPI: parcels, audit status, area summaries, registry comparison, worklist, exports (CSV/GeoJSON)
- Typed tool layer over those endpoints
- LLM agent that plans over the tools; must handle every "Comparable Analysis Request" in the brief
- Every agent answer cites the parcels//rows it used — no unsourced claims
**Gate:** all six brief queries answered end-to-end; each answer traceable to tool calls.

### Phase 8 — React + Leaflet Frontend
**Agent:** `frontend-engineer` · **Skills:** `react-map-ui`
- Map coloured by **agreement status**, not just crop class
- Village audit panel: declared vs satellite-estimated area, delta highlighted
- Field worklist with a budget slider (N visits → expected corrections)
- The Leakage Ladder shown in-product as the honesty credential
- The 4 unlabelled villages as the inference demo
- Conversational panel wired to AI-5
**Gate:** runs from a clean clone with documented commands; every number on screen comes
from the API, none hard-coded.

### Phase 9 — Validation Report & Demo
**Agent:** `validation-reporter`
- Overall + per-class precision/recall/F1, confusion matrix, under every split regime
- Per-class metrics annotated with effective n (capture events), not just record count
- Embedding-source and model-head comparison tables
- Error analysis: which classes confuse, with representative parcels
- Demo script and recorded walkthrough
**Gate:** report states the headline number as capture-event-grouped, shows the random-split
number beside it, and explains the difference.

## 8. Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| FTW model generalises poorly to smallholder Indian fields | Medium | High | Fall back to village-constrained Voronoi parcels (built and verified in Phase 2); report delineation quality honestly as a finding |
| FTW **under**-segments, merging many parcels into one field | Medium | High | The naive Phase 2 gate would pass this. Revised gate measures `one_point_per_field_rate`; `field_id` is unioned into the leakage group key; area aggregated at field level where shared |
| No cadastral geometry from FarmwiseAI | Medium | Medium | AI-1 exists precisely for this; it is the primary path, not the fallback |
| No survey photographs supplied | High | Low | AI-6 is explicitly conditional; the audit works without it |
| MIL does not beat per-parcel baselines | Medium | Low | Attention weights retain audit value; we report the negative result |
| GEE quota / export limits on a taluk-scale AOI | Medium | Medium | Tile the AOI; embeddings are annual and small (64 bands at 10 m) |
| Presto integration cost | Medium | Medium | GSE alone is sufficient for the core; Presto is the comparison arm, time-boxed |
| Audit framing reads as criticism of FarmwiseAI's data | Low | High | Frame as: DCS is a national-scale achievement; provenance artefacts are inherent to field programmes at that scale, and no downstream ML detects them today. That gap is the opportunity. |
| Scope creep across six AI components | High | High | Phase gates. No phase opens before its predecessor's gate passes. |

## 11. Rules of engagement for agents

1. **`plan.md` is authoritative.** Re-read it when a phase opens. If the work requires
   changing it, stop and escalate — that is an architecture decision.
2. **No number without a script.** Any figure that reaches a document must be regenerable
   by a committed script writing into `docs/evidence/`.
3. **No result without its split regime.** Naming the split is mandatory. See
   the `leakage-protocol` skill.
4. **Never claim >95% accuracy.** If a run produces it, that is evidence of leakage;
   find it and report it.
5. **Provenance or it does not exist.** Any acquired file lacking a `PROVENANCE.md` entry
   is not usable.
6. **Report negative results.** A failed idea, documented, is a deliverable.
7. **Escalate, don't guess**, when a decision changes architecture, scope, or the
   thesis in §1.
8. **Respect the phase gate.** Do not begin a phase whose predecessor has not passed.
